Terms of sale

What you are buying

A downloadable dataset (CSV, and XLSX where the product page says so), compiled from public records or verified by hand against live business sources, exactly as described on its product page: its source, its build date, its row count, and its limits. The free sample on every product page is drawn from the same file you receive.

Delivery

Files are delivered by email to the address used at checkout, through a download link that stays good for 72 hours. If a delivery fails, write to hello@parcelreadydata.com and we will resend it by hand. Membership products are billed monthly through Stripe and the current refreshed file is delivered by email on every billing cycle while the membership is active. Cancel any time in the receipt link Stripe sends; cancellation stops future billing and future refresh deliveries.

Use

Your purchase licenses the data for your own business use: outreach, analysis, and client work you perform yourself. It does not license resale or redistribution of the raw file, in whole or in part, as a competing dataset.

The label promise

The promise attaches to the label, not to freshness. If a pack does not match what its page promised (the wrong file, a file that will not open, counts that do not match the label, or a field marked verified that was not), tell us within 30 days of purchase and we will fix it or refund that pack. Ordinary staleness is not covered: public records change after the pull date, and some addresses and emails will go bad over time. The answer to that is the published refresh schedule, and the membership, whose members always hold the current file. A field left blank was not verified, and we say so on the label rather than guess.

Data honesty

Public records change. Every pack carries its data as of date and refreshes on its published schedule, always inside the 30 day rule: lead packs are fully re-verified monthly, and code cases refresh weekly. We do not sell fields we could not verify, and we publish match rates where a file depends on matching.
