Honest limits

Built from the Harris County Appraisal District's published ownership files, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. The population is every owner record with a deed transfer date on or before December 31, 2005, across all market property classes. The exempt division (government, religious, charitable, and educational institutions) is excluded: those are not sellable owners on an investor list. Deed transfer dates are the appraisal district's recorded new owner dates; the district's legacy conversion date appears on pre-digital transfers and is counted in the open (42,711 records carry it), never hidden.
