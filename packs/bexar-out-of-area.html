Honest limits

Built from the Texas Geographic Information Office (TxGIO) StratMap Land Parcels file for Bexar County (2026 collection, county file dated August 4, 2026), a republication of the Bexar Central Appraisal District roll, downloaded from TxGIO's DataHub, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. The file's published situs city and ZIP fields are empty; property city and ZIP are parsed from the appraisal district's own situs address string under a strict pattern, and address strings that do not match the pattern carry no parsed geography. Segmentation follows the standing rules: mailing geography decides first, an identified out-of-state value segments on its own, and the out-of-county segment is Texas mailing ZIPs outside the county's own ZIP set. Records whose geography cannot be identified (665 in the test population) are counted and not shipped. Year built, deed dates, and years owned are not published by this source and are never estimated. Values are the roll's market values, not sale prices.
