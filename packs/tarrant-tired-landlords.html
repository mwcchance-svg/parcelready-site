Honest limits

Built from the Tarrant County Tax Office's published weekly tax roll, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. Owners are matched across the roll on normalized name and mailing address. An owner qualifies holding five or more residential properties with at least half their deed dates on or before December 31, 2015. The county's pre-system placeholder deed date (December 31, 1900) counts toward the long-held half as a genuinely old transfer and is disclosed here and in the build record.
