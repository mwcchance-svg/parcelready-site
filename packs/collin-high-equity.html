Honest limits

Built from the Collin Central Appraisal District's official dataset as published on the Texas Open Data Portal, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. Equity on this pack is a modeled estimate, never a verified figure: the original loan is modeled at 80 percent of value, 30 year fixed at 6.5 percent, amortized from the deed date to the pull date (November 23, 2026), and a record qualifies when the estimated equity reaches 65 percent or more of value. No lender, lien, or mortgage record was checked for any record. Records without a deed date or a value are excluded. Values are the district's prior-year appraised market values, not sale prices.
