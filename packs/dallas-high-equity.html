Honest limits

Built from the Dallas Central Appraisal District's published ownership files, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. Equity on this pack is a modeled estimate, never a verified figure: the original loan is modeled at 80 percent of current appraised value, 30 year fixed at 6.5 percent, amortized from the deed transfer date to the roll data date (October 12, 2026), and a record qualifies when the estimated equity reaches 65 percent or more of current value. No lender, lien, or mortgage record was checked for any record. Records without a deed transfer date or a value are excluded, because the model has nothing honest to amortize. Values are county appraised values, not sale prices. If this pack does not match what its page promised, tell us within 30 days and we will fix it or refund the pack.
