What is inside

Every row, built from the official source

  • Owner name and owner mailing address, city, state, and ZIP
  • Property address, city, and ZIP
  • Latest appraised market value
  • Deed transfer date and years owned (the model's amortization start)
  • Year built and living area square feet (beds and baths are not keyed in the district's published files and ship blank, never estimated)
  • HCAD account number on every record, for verification

Honest limits

Built from the Harris County Appraisal District's published ownership files, not scraped and not resold from another list vendor. Equity on this pack is a modeled estimate, never a verified figure: the original loan is modeled at 80 percent of current appraised value, 30 year fixed at 6.5 percent, amortized from the deed transfer date to the roll date, and a record qualifies when the estimated equity reaches 65 percent or more of current value. No lender, lien, or mortgage record was checked for any record. Records with placeholder deed dates are excluded, because an amortization horizon computed off a conversion date is fabricated precision. Values are county appraised values, not sale prices.
