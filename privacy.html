Privacy

What we collect

When you request a free sample we collect your business name, email, and the trade, state, and metro you asked about, so we can send the sample and follow up about the pack. When you buy, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details; we never see or store your card number. We receive your email and purchase record so we can deliver your file.

What we do with it

Sample requests go to our follow up list, used for the sample itself and occasional notes about the packs you asked about. Purchase records are kept for delivery, membership refreshes, and bookkeeping.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or trade your information. A data company that sold its own customers' details would not deserve customers. We do not add buyers or sample requesters to any dataset we sell.

Your call

Ask at hello@parcelreadydata.com and we will remove your sample request details from the follow up list, or tell you exactly what we hold. Purchase records we must keep for tax and accounting stay for that purpose only.
