Plainly stated

What ParcelReady will not do

NO

No guessed fields. If an email, phone, or owner could not be verified, the field ships blank. Padding a file with invented data is how list sellers burn their buyers, and it is not done here.

NO

No scraped directories sold as verified. Trade rows are checked on the business's own website. County rows come from the official public source, named on the label.

NO

No hidden match rates. Where records are matched across sources, the rate is printed on the product, and unmatched records ship flagged, never filled in.

NO

No renting your details. A sample request is read by the ParcelReady team and used to send your sample and shape the build order. It is never sold, rented, or added to a pack.

NO

No meters, ever. Nothing is charged per record, per export, per lookup, or per document, and there are no credits to expire unused. The price on the page is the whole price.

NO

Never priced per contact. A pack is a file of records from public sources with its pull date printed. It is never priced per homeowner contact, and never metered by whether a phone gets answered.

The standard

How a ParcelReady row earns its place

STEP 01

Verified at the source

Trade pack rows are checked one by one against the business's own website. County packs are built from the appraisal district, the tax roll, and the city's own published data, never from a resold scrape.

STEP 02

Blank means unverified

If a field could not be verified, it ships blank, never guessed. Emails are transcribed exactly as the business prints them, with the source page recorded next to each one.

STEP 03

Limits on the label

Every product prints its source, its build date, its match rates, and what the data cannot tell you. The honest limits section is part of the product, not fine print.

Said out loud

Seven things we can say that the platforms cannot

Every claim below is checkable on this site before you pay: in the samples, on the labels, and in the terms.

  1. A one time price, printed on the page. No subscription required to buy a dataset, and no signup to see what anything costs.
  2. Every count verified and printed before purchase. Rows, published emails, confirmed forms, phones: the numbers sit on the pack page, not in a sales call.
  3. The pull date on every pack, compiled from the county sources directly, not licensed from a bureau layer and described in months.
  4. The pack arrives built. No list building, stacking, or tracing labor on your side. We do the work; you get the leads.
  5. The sample is the product. Real rows, the same fields as the pack, a standardized percentage of the set, before any payment.
  6. A county that fails the standard is skipped, and named as skipped, rather than sold anyway.
  7. Nothing to cancel. A one time pack is a file, not a service quietly billing. Memberships cancel from the receipt link, and their refresh terms sit on the same page as the price.
